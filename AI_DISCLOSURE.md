## 3. Computer Vision and Machine Learning

**Google MediaPipe supplies the pretrained machine-learning models.** AEGIS uses `@mediapipe/tasks-vision` version **0.10.32**, Face Landmarker and Pose Landmarker Lite, with bundled local JavaScript/WebAssembly and `.task` assets. The team selected and experimented with MediaPipe; it did **not** train these pretrained Google models. These are landmark models, not AEGIS-trained fatigue, stress, emotion or diagnostic classifiers. No generative model runs in the camera pipeline.

The geometric methods and engineering thresholds are application logic implemented with AI coding assistance under human direction. Calling them application-specific does not claim the team invented EAR or joint-angle mathematics. `PERCLOS-inspired` is an engineering proxy, not a validated implementation of a clinical PERCLOS standard. Eye closure and blinking do not diagnose fatigue, emotions, stress or psychological conditions. Projected elbow cycles do not measure muscle mass, bone density or injuries. Camera/viewpoint, lighting, tracking gaps, calibration and browser timing limit the measurements; microgravity performance is unvalidated.

The camera module is optional and processes frames locally. It does not upload or persist video, input pixels or raw session landmarks. Saved physical summaries include a sampled angle history; visual summaries include aggregate metrics/intervals. Tracking coverage is time coverage, not diagnostic confidence. Visual coverage excludes the calibration interval, which is separately displayed. A hidden tab stops the camera as a privacy safeguard and is distinguished from user Stop and an unexpected stream end.

The [asset manifest](public/models/vision/manifest.json) records upstream URLs, byte sizes and SHA-256 values for two models and four runtime assets. [Google/MediaPipe notices](public/models/vision/NOTICE.txt) and the [bundled license](public/models/vision/LICENSE) remain unchanged. ## 4. Explainable Health Algorithms

AEGIS primarily uses deterministic, inspectable rules, not a proprietary trained clinical neural network. Codex assisted implementation and test development. The assessment engine projects canonical observations into modalities, separates subjects and origins, checks quality/freshness and compares compatible units, sources, device/protocol labels and conditions. Qualified baselines use the median of three earlier distinct compatible sessions; the current session is excluded. Missing, incompatible, invalid, future or stale inputs produce explicit limitations rather than invented healthy values.

Multimodal findings describe changed sleep, fatigue and reaction/typing indicators, with optional physiology/eyelid context. Environmental rules describe temporal/location association with recorded symptoms. These use declared demonstration choices, not clinical cutoffs or causal proof. There is no universal stress percentage or psychological diagnosis. Clinical decision support exposes evidence and safe verification steps; a person acknowledges, reviews, records follow-up and resolves cases. It does not prescribe treatment or autonomously control life support.

The source of these rules is the authored AEGIS engineering protocol. NASA/other background references do not validate their numerical thresholds. Existing numeric rules, comparison windows and assessment algorithms are unchanged by this metadata update.

## 8. Scientific and Technical Limitations

- Mission, sensor adapters and scenario baselines generate labeled synthetic fixtures. Their implementation/data definitions were AI-assisted; they are deterministic demonstration data, not NASA measurements or clinically validated physiology simulations.
- Manual/cognitive observations and optional camera model estimates retain separate source, quality, subject, time and origin metadata. Observed does not mean clinically validated or device-calibrated.
- No verified physical medical-device connection, clinical diagnostic accuracy, prescribed treatment or medical certification is claimed. ECG/ultrasound examination records are reports, not automated image/waveform interpretation.
- Camera estimates depend on conditions and tracking quality. They do not infer identity, emotions or psychological diagnoses. All core workflows remain usable with the camera off.
- Core operation uses local rules and bundled assets, without a required third-party CDN or remote health API. AI tools used to develop the software are distinct from models executed by the application.
- Local storage is a prototype persistence mechanism, not encrypted clinical infrastructure. Demo role switching is not authentication/authorization. Same-device users or scripts with access to the origin may access stored records; clearing browser data loses them. Do not enter private medical information into the public demo.
- HTTPS/CSP/permissions headers and validated record handling reduce some risks; they are not a comprehensive security audit, regulatory certification or a guarantee of privacy.
